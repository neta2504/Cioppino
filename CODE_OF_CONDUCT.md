# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, and deliberate
  disruption.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's maintainer contact
or GitHub reporting tools. Do not publish sensitive evidence in an issue.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other spaces where someone is representing the Cioppino project.
